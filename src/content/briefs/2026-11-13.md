## TL;DR

- Two papers from 11 Nov study our pipeline shape. One benchmarks 12 video models at turning a coded draft video into a styled one: Wan VACE keeps the draft's structure well and is the worst at replacing the draft's look.
- `unity-comfy` The three runner faults from last run are fixed and tested (fake-server tests 5 of 5, integration 10 of 10). M1 batch 02 stands at 7 of 12 templates passing.
- ComfyUI 0.45.0 is out (10 Nov) and is now the upgrade target.

## Research: what's new

- `unity-comfy` WorldDraft (arXiv 2611.16217): 432 paired cases, drafts from gray clay to previs detail. Wan2.1-VACE-14B keeps structure at 60.97 (best open model 63.28) and scores lowest of 12 on the prompted look (60.86); draft colours that conflict with the prompt survive 64% of the time. More detailed drafts help structure and hurt look for 9 of 10 open models. This supports a look-free control pass (depth or clay) and gives us an adherence metric set. Caveat: their Wan is 14B with an RGB draft; ours is 1.3B with depth.
- `unity-comfy` VibePrevisBench (arXiv 2611.15789): 11 coding agents build graybox previs in Blender; the best scores 77.7 of 100. Every agent gets camera height wrong on 32–41% of shots, and viewing their own renders does not help them notice. An agent-built blockout needs rule checks on camera and object data, which Unity can export.
- `unity-comfy` Releases: ComfyUI 0.45.0 (SeedVR2 VAE and video-decode memory changes; nothing on MPS), templates 0.11.107, comfy-cli 1.26.0. No new open Wan or LTX weights found.
- `agent-directed` User study (arXiv 2611.12605, 30 people): letting the creator lock named facts that later stages must keep raised intent preservation from 3.90 to 5.33 on a 7-point scale. Suggests gates that lock fields, not whole documents.
- `agent-directed` Judge audits (arXiv 2611.13596, grid-world data): one judge accepted 75% of true successes but rejected only 50% of matched failures; another 25% and 92%. Audit on matched good/bad pairs, not one accuracy figure. Plan got a Rev 4 note; M0 unchanged.

## Implementation progress

- `unity-comfy` Runner (ComfyUI 0.38.2, torch 2.12.1):
  - A cancel is confirmed; if the job keeps running, the server is killed by port pid and restarted.
  - A "stalled" verdict requires the server process to be idle.
  - A timeout with advancing steps is labelled "too slow", with a projection.
  - On real jobs so far: only the label and the confirmed cancel. MoGe panorama, which showed the other two faults last run, passed in 59 s today (third different outcome on the same input).
- `unity-comfy` Batch results:
  - Passed: SAM3 video (both inputs), Wan Fun Camera on its sample image (18.1 min), SDPose video on the Unity clip (104 s), MoGe panorama.
  - SeedVR2 video: too slow on both inputs (140 s per step, about 28 min projected, 20 min limit).
  - SDPose video at the template's default size fails on our 832x480 clip with a libx264 error; the scaled height would be odd (591). At 832 px it passes.
  - SAM3 on the Unity clip passes with nearly empty masks (the template's prompt is "unicorn"). Our check does not catch this yet.
- `unity-comfy` The validator wrongly rejected a moving pose video as frozen; fixed (tests 11 of 11).
- `unity-comfy` comfy-cli 1.26.0 converts all 24 tested templates exactly as 1.23.0, so our workaround stays.
