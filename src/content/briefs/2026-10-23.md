## TL;DR

- Every M0 exit item now has a measured result. The last three:
  - adherence checks for Wan (depth) and TripoSplat (silhouette);
  - E2, the comparison against the browser's Save (API), now run headless;
  - P1, a scripted model download with checksums.
- PyTorch 2.15.0 (test build, in a separate venv) fixes our MPS silent failure: TripoSplat's
  preview works at full density. Wan VACE 17f also drops from 48.5 s to 25.0 s warm (\~1.9×), with
  the same adherence.
- The Wan adherence check works on this scene. It passes 35/35 good clips and fails 9/9 clips
  with broken control, but it misses a small (96 px) layout shift.
- The 20 Oct run failed in 3 min (API connection errors before any work). Nothing was lost.

## Research: what's new

- ComfyUI is up to 0.42.1. None of 0.39–0.42 fixes anything on Mac; we stay on 0.38.2 for now.
  comfy-cli 1.23.0 and comfy-sdk 0.5.0 are out: a reused submit key is rejected, not replayed,
  which matches our runner's design.
- LTX black output on Mac: a reported workaround flag (`--use-split-cross-attention`, #15818) and
  an unmerged fix (#15819). ltx-2-mlx 0.18.0 is an MLX port that runs LTX IC-LoRAs without
  PyTorch, a possible Mac route to Layout to Render.

## Implementation progress

- P1, `scripts/fetch-model.py`: downloaded Depth Anything 3 (small + large, 1.47 GB) with sha256
  from the HF API. Resume, over-budget refusal, gated (401) and corrupt-file handling all worked.
- Wan adherence, `scripts/depth-adherence.py`: DA3 small re-estimates depth on the output and
  compares it with Unity's depth pass.
  - MAE ≈ 0.03 on good clips vs 0.14–0.15 at VACE strength 0.4/0.0 and 0.068 with a mirrored
    control.
  - Structure collapses between strength 0.8 and 0.4: it's a step, not a gradual slope.
  - The score is now stored in K4 manifests.
  - Lab note: [Experiment 005](../../experiments/2026-10-23-wan-depth-adherence-and-p1-download/)
- TripoSplat adherence, `scripts/splat-silhouette.py`: matched subjects score IoU 0.51,
  mismatched 0.19. Tested on two subjects, including a new Unity mannequin splat.
- E2, `scripts/frontend-api-export.py`: drives the real frontend in headless Chrome.
  - The M0 workflows are clean.
  - Of 20 templates: 12 identical, 6 with harmless differences, 2 that matter.
    - The frontend drops a bypassed node's prompt passthrough.
    - comfy-cli injects a SAM3 bounding box the user never set.
  - comfy-cli 1.23 produced exactly the same conversions as 1.22.
- Torch 2.15 lab note: [Experiment 008](../../experiments/2026-10-23-torch-2-15-mps-inv-and-wan/)
- Unity wasn't run; all inputs came from existing captures.

## Risks / blocked

- The adherence thresholds were set on one scene (Wan) and two subjects (TripoSplat). They are
  not held-out results yet.
- Torch 2.15 is a test-index build, and Python/av/numpy versions also differ from the main venv,
  so the speedup isn't attributed to torch alone yet.
- One E2 result came from the headless frontend and wasn't confirmed in a normal browser: it lost
  the prompt passthrough of a bypassed node.
