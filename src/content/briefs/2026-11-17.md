## TL;DR

- Correction to the last brief: the SAM3 masks on the Unity clip were not "nearly empty". Scored against Unity's own segmentation pass, they select the mannequin (median IoU 0.86). SAM3 video on Unity input is a real pass.
- `unity-comfy` Two checks built and tested: jobs whose video would have an odd frame size are refused before they run, and masks are checked for coverage and against Unity ground truth. M1 batch 02 stands at 8 of 12 templates passing.
- `unity-comfy` MoGe panorama failed 5 of 5 runs. The hang is located: a sparse solver with no iteration limit.
- `agent-directed` New benchmark of agents producing short dramas (240 runs): the agents that score best on quality often deliver incomplete videos. Plan got a Rev 5 note.

## Research: what's new

- `agent-directed` VAHBench (arXiv 2611.20535): five short-drama briefs, 48 agent setups. Claude Code: completeness 0.814, quality 0.513. Codex: quality 0.616, completeness 0.136. Ten real seconds looped to fill 60 s score 0.057, so a file of the right length proves little. Purpose-built video agents (ViMax and two others) delivered least (0.125–0.375).
- `agent-directed` VIVID (arXiv 2611.20688): a harness that lets coding agents inspect video and keep observations with timecodes. Claude Code goes from 32.4% to 55.1% on 100 editing tasks for 10.7% more tokens; removing the review of rendered output costs 14.6 points.
- `unity-comfy` comfy-mcp 0.11.0 (16 Nov), first release since August: a video contact-sheet tool, a flag for paid nodes, a per-node failure report. It overlaps part of our runner but describes no stall or content checks. Not installed.
- `unity-comfy` Otherwise quiet: no ComfyUI release since 0.45.0; no new open Wan or LTX weights or blockout-to-video paper found.

## Implementation progress

- `unity-comfy` Odd-size check: SDPose multi-person video on the Unity clip failed after 112 s at the video save (frames 1024x591). The runner now predicts that size and refused the same job before queueing. Tests 9 of 9. Sizes it cannot predict are let through.
- `unity-comfy` Mask checks: a coverage check (catches empty or whole-frame masks) and a script that scores masks per frame against Unity's segmentation pass. SAM3 prompted "unicorn", "person" and "car" selected the mannequin each time (median IoU 0.86–0.87), so the text prompt did not decide the result in this scene.
- `unity-comfy` SDPose multi-person video: passes on its sample clip (324 s). On the Unity clip at 832 px, 8 of 81 frames have no skeleton, so it is rejected.
- `unity-comfy` MoGe panorama, five runs:
  - 3 default runs: hang at the same merge step until the 15 min limit.
  - Smaller merge size: finishes, then "MoGe produced an empty mesh".
  - The template's own panorama: segfault in torch grid_sample after 27 s.
  - Its one pass (13 Nov) is not reproduced.
- `unity-comfy` Runner: the three hangs ignored the cancel; the runner restarted the server each time (first real use). A job whose steps stop is now labelled "stuck", not "too slow". Fake-server tests 6 of 6, integration 10 of 10.
